Program 02 · Minority stakes

Investment from our own capital

We invest from our own balance sheet, which means no fund timetable and no outside investors to satisfy. We take minority positions, expect founders to keep control, and insist both sides are separately advised by their own solicitors before anything is signed.

  • Case by case
  • Established businesses
  • Individually negotiated
  • Full diligence
